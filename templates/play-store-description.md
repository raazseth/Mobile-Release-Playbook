# Google Play Store Listing Metadata Template

Use this template to format store text metadata for **Google Play Console** in full compliance with Google Play Store Listing policies.

---

# Google Play Metadata Fields

### 1. App Title — Max 30 Characters
```text
[FitnessTracker Pro: Gym Log]
```
*(Character count check: 27 / 30 characters max)*

### 2. Short Description — Max 80 Characters
```text
[Track gym workouts, log exercises, & monitor strength progress effortlessly.]
```
*(Character count check: 77 / 80 characters max)*

### 3. Long Description — Max 4,000 Characters (Keyword Density 2-3%)
```text
Take your workout routine to the next level with FitnessTracker Pro — the ultimate gym log and workout tracker engineered for serious athletes and beginners alike.

FitnessTracker Pro makes it simple to log gym workouts, track exercise sets and reps, monitor weight progress, and reach your fitness goals faster.

CORE FEATURES:

• LOG WORKOUTS EFFORTLESSLY: Track sets, reps, weights, and rest timers with our intuitive workout tracker interface.
• INTERACTIVE ANALYTICS: Visualize your strength progress over time with detailed workout charts and personal records.
• CUSTOM ROUTINES: Build custom gym workout routines or choose from expert-designed strength training programs.
• OFFLINE SYNC: Log workouts anywhere, anytime — zero internet connection required.
• PRIVACY FOCUSED: Your fitness data stays private on your device and secure cloud backups.

WHY CHOOSE FITNESSTRACKER PRO?
Whether your goal is muscle building, weight loss, powerlifting, or general health tracking, FitnessTracker Pro provides the tools you need to log workouts accurately and stay motivated.

Privacy Policy: https://example.com/privacy
```

# Operational Verification Checklist

- [ ] **Title ≤ 30 Characters**: App Title verified strictly within 30c boundary.
- [ ] **Short Description ≤ 80 Characters**: Short Description verified strictly within 80c boundary.
- [ ] **No Prohibited Claims**: Title free of "Free", "Sale", "Top #1 App", or "Download Now".
- [ ] **Keyword Density 2-3%**: Long description maintains natural keyword reading flow without keyword stuffing.
- [ ] **Account Deletion Link**: Public web link for account/data deletion provided.
